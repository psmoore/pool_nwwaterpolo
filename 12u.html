NorthwestWater Polo Club

12u 3x-a-week

12u boys and girls, intermediate to advanced, three practices a week.

Hour-and-a-half practices twice a week plus a shorter weekend practice, for motivated novice through experienced players. Conditioning with swimming and egg-beater drills first, then shooting drills, front court work and counter attack drills.

  • Cost: $250/month
  • USAWP membership level: Bronze (local scrimmages), Silver (travel tournaments, not Jr. Olympics) or Gold (travel tournaments and Jr. Olympics)
  • Coaches: Andrew Falaniko and Daniel Kavesh
