Trip planning

  • 14u and up, travel tournaments: fees include tournament entry, hotels and team vans at the destination. Families book flights, with direction from the coaches so the team can travel together. Estimated fee $400 per athlete per tournament.
  • Meals: athletes pay for their own, around $40–60 per day. Tell the coach about allergies or dietary needs.
  • Meeting: at the airline's check-in counter, usually about an hour and a half before the flight.
  • Pack light: one swim/water polo bag. NWPC suit (or an all-black polo suit), NWPC shirt or hoodie, towel, running shoes, water bottle, goggles, medications, phone charger.
  • Coming home: meet your athlete at the gate (with the airline's escort pass) or outside security.

Full details on the club's Tournament Travel Information page.
