Welcome to Northwest Water Polo Club

Welcome! Northwest Water Polo Club (NWPC) is the premier water polo organization serving Seattle, Bellevue, Mercer Island, and the Greater Seattle area. A non-profit, our mission is to develop young athletes through year-round training, competitive tournaments, and a commitment to excellence both in and out of the pool.

Programs for All Ages and Skill Levels We offer inclusive programs for boys and girls ages 5 and up, welcoming athletes at every stage, from beginners to elite competitors.

Excellence in Athletics and Academics NWPC athletes consistently stand out in the classroom and in competition, with a strong tradition of Academic All-American honorees (minimum 3.5 GPA).

What's different in this preview

Today on northwestwaterpoloclub.com

  • The practice schedule is a paragraph per team on the Our Schedule page, and one team's line names no pool for two of its days.
  • Start dates, holidays and breaks are on a different page (the tournament schedule), so a parent reads both.
  • The site's Calendar page shows no practices.
  • When a night moves, parents hear by message, and the page has to be edited by hand.

In this preview

  • One calendar, kept by the coaches, with each practice in its pool and lanes, holidays and breaks already left out.
  • Each team has its own page and calendar, and every day and time on the site is read from that calendar.
  • Parents follow their team: a change email, and the practices in Google, Apple or Outlook Calendar, always current.
  • The pools see the same bookings: see the Phantom Lake and Woodridge previews.
